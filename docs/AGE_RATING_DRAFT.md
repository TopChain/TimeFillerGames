# TimeFillerGames Release 1 — Age / Content Rating Working Draft

Use this document while completing App Store Connect and Google Play Console. It is derived from actual Release 1 behavior and current August 2026 store definitions. The store-generated rating remains authoritative, and answers must be rechecked against the final signed binaries/content immediately before submission.

## Release-positioning decision

TimeFillerGames Release 1 is **not** submitted as Apple “Made for Kids” and is **not** marketed as a child-directed app. The dedicated Kids context is removed from Release 1. Classroom is a general group context, not a claim that the service is designed primarily for children.

The product can still be used by families/classes subject to the account owner’s final policies and local requirements. Do not select child age groups in Google Play merely because classroom use exists; selecting a child target audience triggers Google Families requirements.

## Apple App Store Connect — working answers

Apple determines the age rating from content descriptors, capabilities, controls, and their frequency. Current Apple definitions classify **Contests** as events where users compete for rankings, rewards, or personal goals, including skill/trivia competitions. Release 1 uses rankings/podiums as a normal game outcome, so answer **Frequent Contests** rather than minimizing the competitive feature.

### In-app controls

- Parental Controls: **No**
- Age Assurance: **No**

### Capabilities

- Unrestricted Web Access: **No** — there is no embedded general-purpose browser. First-party legal/support/account links may open web pages, but users cannot freely browse arbitrary sites inside TimeFillerGames.
- Social Media: **No**
- Social Media Disabled for Users Under 13: **No / not applicable**
- Messaging and Chat: **No** — Release 1 has no general chat/messaging feature. Quick Draw guesses are game submissions; public live guess streaming is disabled.
- User-Generated Content: **Working answer: No**, based on Apple’s capability definition referring to broad distribution of user-created content. Release 1 drawings/guesses/nicknames are ephemeral room-scoped game inputs rather than a public content feed, profile/content-sharing network, or broadly distributed UGC system. **Re-read the exact App Store Connect questionnaire wording at submission; if Apple presents room-scoped multiplayer drawings as qualifying UGC, answer Yes rather than understating the feature.**
- Advertising: **No**

### Mature themes / language

Curated Release 1 prompt and word banks intentionally avoid mature material.

- Profanity or Crude Humor: **None in curated app content**. Users can enter nicknames/Quick Draw guesses, but public live guess streaming is disabled. If App Store Connect explicitly asks about possible user-entered language rather than developer-provided content, answer conservatively from the final moderation behavior.
- Horror or Fear Themes: **None**
- Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use/References: **None**
- Mature or Suggestive Themes: **None**

### Medical / wellness

- Health or Wellness Topics: **None**
- Medical or Treatment Information: **None**

### Sexuality / nudity

- Sexual Content or Nudity: **None**
- Graphic Sexual Content and Nudity: **None**

### Violence / weapons

- Cartoon or Fantasy Violence: **None**
- Realistic Violence: **None**
- Prolonged Graphic/Sadistic Violence: **None**
- Guns or Other Weapons: **None in curated Release 1 content**

### Chance-based activities

- Gambling: **None** — no real-money/redeemable wagering.
- Simulated Gambling: **None** — Bingo uses server-random draws but has no wager, casino economy, betting mechanic, or redeemable stake.
- Loot Boxes: **None**
- Contests: **Frequent** — normal gameplay includes competition/rankings/podium results.

### Expected Apple global rating

**Expected: 13+** on iOS/iPadOS/macOS 26-era rating rules, because Apple lists **Frequent Contests** under the 13+ rating category.

This is an engineering expectation, not an override. Submit accurate answers and accept the rating generated by App Store Connect unless a legal/EULA minimum age requires a higher manual override.

Older Apple OS versions may display the prior global rating system; App Store Connect applies the required region/OS-specific mappings.

## Google Play — IARC Content Rating working posture

Google Play requires an IARC rating for every app. Complete the questionnaire accurately from the final Release 1 build; regional ratings can differ.

Working content answers:

- Violence: **No**
- Sexual content/nudity: **No**
- Fear/horror: **No**
- Drugs/alcohol/tobacco: **No**
- Gambling/wagering: **No**
- Simulated gambling: **No**
- Real-money purchases / loot boxes: **No in Release 1**
- Advertising: **No**
- General chat/social network: **No**
- User interaction / multiplayer: **Yes where the IARC questionnaire asks about users interacting with other users**, because Players join multiplayer rooms, see participant nicknames/avatars, and participate in shared game state.
- User-generated content/content sharing: answer from the exact questionnaire wording. Room-scoped drawings/nicknames are user-created game inputs, but there is no public feed/gallery. Do not answer “No” if the questionnaire explicitly includes temporary multiplayer drawings/content.

The IARC result must be submitted and kept current whenever app content/features change.

## Google Play target audience — account-owner/legal decision

Do **not** select child age groups simply to broaden discoverability. Google states that selecting any audience that includes children subjects the app to Families requirements.

Release 1 engineering/marketing posture is **not child-directed**. The final target-age groups should therefore be selected only after the account owner/legal reviewer decides who the product is intentionally designed and marketed for in each launch region.

Safe engineering constraints already in place:

- dedicated Kids context unavailable
- no custom participant photos
- no advertising SDK
- no cross-app tracking SDK
- public Quick Draw guess stream disabled
- built-in avatars
- temporary rooms/data lifecycle
- Host moderation controls
- first-party Privacy/Terms/Support/Account Data pages

These constraints reduce risk but do not substitute for the account owner’s target-audience declaration.
